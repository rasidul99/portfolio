About me

Hi, I’m Rasidul Islam — a Product-focused Designer & Developer from Bangladesh.

I started my journey in design and have grown into someone who enjoys turning ideas and business problems into simple, engaging digital products. My work blends UI/UX design, visual design, branding, and modern development to create experiences that are both beautiful and useful.
